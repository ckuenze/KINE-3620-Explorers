Reaction Time Lab

How fast are your reflexes, Hoos? Run three short experiments, earn your stat line, and see how your reactions connect to agility.

What you will do (about 15 minutes)

Experiment 1, Simple. Respond to one signal. Is a sound or a light faster?

Experiment 2, Choices. Respond to 1, 2, 3 or 4 possible signals. What does a decision cost?

Experiment 3, Twists. Add rules, fakes and hints. Which one slows you down most?

Before each experiment you will make a prediction. Your results page then compares your predictions with what happened and shows what your reaction time means on the field or court.

Before you begin

Sit comfortably with the page in front of you. Silence notifications. Use the keyboard if you can, because it is the most consistent. A tap on a touchscreen also works.

For the sound experiment, use the device speakers or wired headphones. Bluetooth headphones add a delay and will make your sound times look slower than they are. No headphones in a noisy room? You can skip the sound block in Experiment 1.

Set your volume so the beep is clear but not loud.

Words you will see

StimulusThe signal you respond to, such as a light or a sound.
Reaction time (RT)The time from the stimulus to your response, in milliseconds (ms). 1000 ms is one second.
MedianThe middle value of your trials. It is not thrown off by one odd trial.
ChoiceA task where you must pick the right response from several options.
AnticipationResponding before the signal, or so early that it must be a guess.
AccuracyHow often you picked the right response.
A note about the numbers. Screens, speakers, and keyboards add a small delay, so your times will be longer than in a research lab. Compare your own conditions with each other on the same device. Do not compare your numbers with a classmate who used a different device.

Combine class results (instructor)

Students copy their results code from the Results tab and submit it, for example in a Canvas quiz or form. Paste the codes here, one per line. Everything is calculated in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
