# Balance and the External Moment Arm

Change how high the center of mass (COM) sits and how far the body leans, and see what happens to the moment arm and to the external torque gravity creates about the base of support.

- **Topic:** Balance, COM height, and gravitational torque
- **Use:** [PRE/IN]
- **Unit / week:** [UNIT]

## Learning objectives

After using this tool, students should be able to:

- Calculate the external moment arm as d = h × sin(θ).
- Calculate the external torque as τ = m × g × d.
- Explain why a taller, more extended posture creates more torque than a crouched one at the same lean angle.
- Relate COM height to ankle vs. hip balance strategies.

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in a web browser.

- Adjust **COM height (h)**, **angle from vertical (θ)**, and **body mass**.
- Try the presets: *Standing tall, slight sway*, *Partial crouch, same sway*, and *Near limit of stability*.
- The **Live calculation** panel shows each step of the moment arm and torque math.
