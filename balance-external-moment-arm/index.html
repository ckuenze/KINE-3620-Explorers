Why this matters

The external torque gravity creates about your base of support depends on both variables — not just how far you've leaned. A tall, extended body (higher h) produces a much larger torque for the exact same lean angle than a lower, more crouched body does. That's part of why a hip strategy — which keeps the COM relatively high — has to work against more torque than an ankle strategy, and why crouching lowers your COM and reduces the torque a given perturbation can create.
